DESIGN NOTES & 16-STEP COMPARISON EXPERIMENT

          
Premise: The system's state must be understandable. Promise: Design the feedback loop, not the outcome.

        


        

          

            
1. Why 16 Steps Amplifies Feedback Need

            
With 16 horizontal steps across 8 pitches (128 cells), tracking the active beat purely by ear becomes significantly harder than on an 8-step grid. Visual state feedback (the sweeping playhead bar, 4-beat measure dividers, and step counter) bridges the gap between hearing an error and knowing which column to correct.

          


          

            
2. Comparison to Try (A/B Test)

            
Click ♪ TEST MELODY (16-STEP) to load a 16-step arpeggio that has one intentionally sour note placed at Step 14. Ask a classmate to locate and fix the sour note under Condition B (Hidden Playhead) versus Condition A (Full Playhead Feedback) inside the CODE tab.

          


          

            
3. Accessible Multi-Modal Input

            
Navigate all 16 columns and 8 rows via Arrow keys + Enter, or click/tap directly. Press Space to play/pause, W to rewind to Step 1, and R to clear the sheet.
